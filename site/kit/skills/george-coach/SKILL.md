# Coach

For any job bigger than one sitting. The coach plans, does the steps, checks each one, and accepts nothing without proof. It also keeps the job alive across sessions.

## Steps

1. **Frame.** Write `projects/<job>.md` from `templates/PROJECT.md`: the goal in one sentence, what done looks like, what is out of scope, who decides (George, or a named helper).
2. **Plan.** Break the job into steps of 30 minutes or less. One checkbox per step. Mark the steps that need George (sign-ups, choices, approvals) with **GEORGE**, and batch his questions so he answers them once.
3. **Do.** Work the steps in order. After each one, run its check (open the page, run the test, read the file back) and tick the box with the proof: a link, a file path, a screenshot path or a command result.
4. **Checkpoint.** Every 30 minutes, george-handoff in checkpoint mode. A fresh session must be able to read the project file and continue.
5. **Review before done.** For anything George will publish or rely on, do a separate review pass with fresh eyes: re-read the goal, check every box's proof, and look for what would embarrass him. If you can, use a different session or agent for this pass; the builder doesn't approve its own work.
6. **Accept.** Done means every box has proof and the result is in the dashboard queue or live. Write the result and the next steps in the handoff.

## When something fails

- Fix the smallest thing that unblocks the step, then re-run its check.
- After three failed tries on the same step, stop and propose a simpler route or a cut in scope to George.
- Never hide a failure. Say what broke, what you tried and what you need.

## Rules

- No step is "done" on your word alone. Proof, or it stays open.
- Drafts only; publishing and sending wait for Approve in the dashboard.
- No made-up facts. `[check]` and ask.
- No keys in chat (george-secrets).

## Output

The project file, kept current, and a closing line: "Done: <goal>. Proof: <links>. Next: <step>."

## Try saying

"Coach this: get the TOI donor thank-you process running from start to finish."

## Make it yours

Change the step size, the review rule or who approves. Keep "proof per box"; it is the part that stops drift.
