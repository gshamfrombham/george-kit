# George's voice

George wrote with stories, humor and Southern specifics long before AI. This skill keeps every draft sounding like him.

## Sources (read before writing)

- `voice/VOICE.md`: the guide George approved.
- `voice/gold/`: his own best posts, verbatim. These are the standard.
- `voice/banned.md`: what never appears (see george-slop-check).

If `voice/VOICE.md` doesn't exist yet, build it first (below) and show George before writing anything for publication.

## Building the voice guide (once, then refine)

1. Collect 5 to 8 of George's own pre-AI posts into `voice/gold/`, verbatim, chosen by George. Good candidates are personal stories, outdoor devotionals and mission-week posts.
2. Note what they do: how he opens (a person, a place, a moment), how he uses humor, his family and Alabama details, how scripture enters the story, his rhythm (long winding lines broken by a short one), words and phrases that are his.
3. Write `voice/VOICE.md` as do and don't lines with a short example of each, quoting only his own sentences.
4. George edits it. His edits win.
5. **Blind test.** Show George one real paragraph of his and one written with this skill, unlabeled, three times. Passing means he can't reliably pick his own.

## Writing in his voice

- Answer the question plainly near the top when the piece is a Bible question (good for search and AI answers), then tell it the way George tells a story.
- Use one real scene or detail he gave you. Never invent an anecdote, a person or a quote.
- Scripture is quoted as text with book, chapter and verse, in his preferred translation (see BRAIN.md; `[check]` if unknown). Never as an image only.
- First person, warm, direct. Short paragraphs, no stacked one-line drama.

## Rules

- Run george-slop-check on every draft before it goes to the dashboard.
- Never write sermon or theological positions for him that he hasn't stated. Summarize his teaching, don't create it.
- No em dashes.

## Make it yours

George should keep adding favorite posts to `voice/gold/` and lines to VOICE.md. The guide gets better every month.
