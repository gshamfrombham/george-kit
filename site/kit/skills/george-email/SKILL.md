# Email

Draft-only email in George's voice.

## Steps

1. **Which identity.** Read `decisions/0004-boundaries.md` (one identity per mailbox). Decide which identity this email belongs to (Author, TOI, G's Plan, CEL) and use that address as the sender in the draft header. If the thread, the recipient and the requested sender don't match one identity, or it belongs to George's work for another company, don't draft it: ask George one question, for example "This looks like work mail; should it be drafted from your work account instead?"
2. **Context.** Read the thread George pasted or shared, and `people/<name>.md` if it exists.
3. **Draft** short: greeting, the point in the first two lines, any specifics, one clear ask or next step, his sign-off from BRAIN.md.
4. **Check facts:** dates, times, links and names come from the thread or the brain. `[check]` the rest.
5. **Deliver** the draft: post it to the dashboard queue as an email item (or give it in chat if George asked for a quick one). After Approve, George or a helper sends it. Whatever a mail connector technically allows, this skill never creates or sends anything in his mailbox without an Approve for that exact draft.
6. Save new facts about the person with george-brain.

## Rules

- Never send, and never create drafts, rules, filters or forwards in his mailbox without Approve, whatever the connector allows.
- One identity per mailbox (`decisions/0004-boundaries.md`). Never draft across identities; ask instead.
- Private matters (pastoral care, donors, family) stay out of logs and the public kit.
- No em dashes; no "I hope this email finds you well."

## Output

Subject line, To and Cc, body, and any [check] items.

## Try saying

"Draft a reply to Pastor Luis confirming the March dates and asking about lodging."

## Make it yours

Put his sign-offs per brand in BRAIN.md, and add people he writes often to `people/`.
