# Tuesday video package

George records. Everything around the recording is ready before he hits the button.

## Inputs

- Topic from `calendar/` or George, and his notes or outline.
- Series (Torchbearers, Systematic Theology Shorts, other) from BRAIN.md.
- `voice/VOICE.md`.

## Steps

1. george-session-start.
2. **Hook (first 15 seconds):** the question or the moment, in words he'd actually say. Two options.
3. **Script:** spoken-length sentences, about 130 to 150 words a minute, matched to the length George wants (default 6 to 9 minutes). Mark where scripture appears and quote it as text with the reference.
4. **For history episodes:** dates, names and events only from sources George trusts, each one listed at the end with `[check]` until he confirms.
5. **Upload pack:** three title options (searchable, honest, no clickbait), description (first two lines carry the answer and a link to the article or site), chapters with times as placeholders, pinned comment, thumbnail words (3 to 5 words), and tags.
6. **Social cut:** a 30 to 60 second short script from the strongest moment, plus captions (george-repurpose).
7. george-slop-check, then george-approval-queue posts one "Tuesday video package" version.

## Rules

- George records and uploads; the package makes upload a paste job.
- No invented history, quotes or numbers.
- No em dashes; titles in plain words.

## Output

Script, hook options, titles, description, chapters, pinned comment, thumbnail words, tags, short script, captions, slop report, as one package in the queue.

## Try saying

"Video package for Torchbearers episode 6 on John Calvin, eight minutes."

## Make it yours

Add his standard intro and outro lines and his preferred video length to BRAIN.md. The skill will use them.
