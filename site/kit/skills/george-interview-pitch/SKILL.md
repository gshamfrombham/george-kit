# Interview pitch

George has done many radio and podcast interviews. This keeps the next ones coming and makes him ready for each.

## Inputs

- His books, topics and past interviews from BRAIN.md and his site's interview posts.
- The show: name, host, audience, recent episodes (from the show's own page; links required).

## Steps

1. **Find fits** (if asked): shows whose recent episodes match his topics. List each with a link to a recent episode and why it fits. No guessed audience sizes.
2. **Pitch** (120 to 180 words): why this show's listeners would care now, one specific angle tied to a recent episode or season, two or three question ideas, one line on who he is, links to his site and a past interview.
3. **One-sheet:** bio (short and long), headshot location, book list, five topics, sample questions, contact path (public site contact only).
4. **Prep notes** after a yes: host background from public sources, likely questions, his three key points, one story for each, scripture references he plans to use (quoted exactly).
5. **Follow-up** drafts: a thank-you after, and a share post when the episode airs (george-repurpose).
6. george-slop-check, then george-approval-queue. Never send.

## Rules

- Facts about hosts and shows only from public pages, with links. `[check]` anything else.
- No invented credentials, sales numbers or endorsements.
- No em dashes.

## Output

Fit list, pitch, one-sheet, prep notes or follow-ups, depending on the ask.

## Try saying

"Pitch me to three Christian podcasts about The Four Faces of Christ."

## Make it yours

Keep a `people/` file for each host with the date, episode link and outcome. Next pitches get easier.
