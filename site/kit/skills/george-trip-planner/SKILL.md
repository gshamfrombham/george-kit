# Trip planner

Trips run on checklists, not memory.

## Inputs

- Dates, destination, team list, partners, purpose, budget limits (from George or the TOI lead).
- `projects/toi/PLAYBOOK.md`, `projects/toi/PRIVACY.md` and `projects/toi/CONSENT.md`, read before writing anything.

## Steps

1. Create `projects/trips/<yyyy-mm>-<place>/` from `templates/PROJECT.md`.
2. **Timeline:** 90, 60, 30, 14, 7 and 1 days out, with tasks under each (documents, travel requirements to verify with official sources, flights, lodging, supplies, team training, partner confirmations). Travel and visa rules are `[check]` against the official government source; never state them from memory.
3. **Team packet:** schedule, contacts list held privately, packing list, what to expect, conduct and safety rules, and the daily rhythm.
4. **Comms plan:** a pre-trip supporter update, a prayer calendar (one focus per day), daily or every-other-day trip notes (george-missions-update), and a post-trip report.
5. **Budget sheet:** categories and who approves spending. No payments by any agent.
6. Add every dated task to `calendar/` and to the dashboard as reminders.
7. george-slop-check on supporter-facing text, then george-approval-queue.

## Rules

- No bookings, payments or sign-ups by the agent. Draft, list and remind only.
- Travel and health requirements only from official sources, marked `[check]` until a person confirms.
- PRIVACY.md and CONSENT.md apply to every document that leaves the team.
- No em dashes.

## Output

Project folder with timeline, team packet, comms plan, budget sheet, and calendar entries.

## Try saying

"Plan the March Cuba trip: team of eight, the 14th to the 21st."

## Make it yours

Save a finished trip as the template for the next one in `projects/trips/TEMPLATE/`.
