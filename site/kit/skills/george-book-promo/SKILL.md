# Book promo

Steady, honest promotion of the books George has already written.

## Inputs

- The book: title, subtitle, description, table of contents, key passages George chooses, buy links (from his site's book pages).
- Any real reviews or endorsements, with their source.

## Jobs this skill does

- **Launch or relaunch plan:** a 6-week calendar of posts, emails, videos and interview pitches (george-interview-pitch), saved in `projects/books/<title>/`.
- **Book page copy** for his site and retail descriptions (george-site-update for the site).
- **Posts:** a passage with a short reflection in his voice, a question from the book, a behind-the-scenes story he gave you.
- **Study or discussion guide:** chapter questions for small groups.
- **Email:** an announcement or a series for his list.

## Steps

1. Read the book materials and `voice/VOICE.md`.
2. Pick the job. Draft.
3. Quote the book exactly; give the page or chapter if known.
4. george-slop-check, then george-approval-queue under brand "Author."

## Rules

- Never invent reviews, star counts, sales numbers, rankings or endorsements. Only quote endorsements that exist, with their source.
- Retail listing changes and ad spending are George's call, done by a person. This skill drafts.
- No em dashes.

## Output

The plan, copy, posts, guide or email, packaged for one approval.

## Try saying

"Plan six weeks of posts for The Four Faces of Christ, one a week."

## Make it yours

Add each book's best passages and real endorsements to `projects/books/<title>/` so every draft starts from them.
